# Free evidence audit v2

This is a new, additive classification route for Qlib missing member sessions.
It preserves `FREE_AUDIT_CLOSURE_20260913.md` and its archived evidence; it does
not overwrite earlier reports or treat their accepted residual as invented proof.

The route uses an exchange calendar and lifecycle evidence first, BaoStock
unadjusted `query_history_k_data_plus` rows as independent provider evidence,
and archived SSE/SZSE records for official confirmation. AKShare may only add a
discovery lead. Tushare is optional and no missing token, credits, or paid
service blocks this route.

`FREE_EVIDENCE_COMPLETE` means the missing-session funnel has no
`PROVIDER_CONFLICT` or `UNEXPLAINED` rows and has `gap_gate=PASS`. It is not
formal qualification, PIT membership confirmation, a promotion decision, or a
permission to train or trade. Formal research remains `BLOCKED_DATA` until its
separate evidence gates are satisfied.

The residual renderer lists only blocking rows, their archived evidence hash,
and the minimum remaining official or lifecycle evidence. It never recommends
purchasing a data source.
