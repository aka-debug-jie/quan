The paired 21-session block bootstrap used 10,000 fixed-seed draws and Holm adjustment across all eight originally registered candidates. None of the four positive-active candidates passed the frozen retention rule: all corresponding 95% daily-active-return intervals cross zero and their Holm-adjusted one-sided p-values are 1.0. AF7 avoidance's +0.52 pp main active CAGR turns to -0.51 pp under doubled friction; conditional reversal remains +1.06 pp under doubled friction and +1.04 pp at T+2, but its interval still crosses zero and absolute CAGR is negative. The filter-only control beats the reversal ranking by 0.64 pp CAGR with much less turnover; because it was registered after seeing prior results, this is a mechanism clue, **not** a new formal positive candidate. The 500,000/5,000,000 CNY scale scenarios are capacity diagnostics only and do not change the eight-candidate family or the no-promotion decision.

The daily AF7 audit illustrates execution drag: 1,407.93 lifetime two-sided turnover, 1,054,786 CNY cumulative costs and -37.09% CAGR. Holding buffers lower turnover but do not meet the preregistered statistical and economic retention conditions. Negative absolute performance does not by itself negate relative comparison; a positive historical active CAGR does not prove alpha.
